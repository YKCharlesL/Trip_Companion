# Your trip app: how to use it

All your bookings, tickets, maps and addresses in one place. **It works without internet** once
it is set up. (Every export also makes a printable copy of this sheet with the link and phone
numbers filled in: `exports/<trip>/How-to-use-<trip>.html`.)

## 1 · Set it up (once, at home on Wi-Fi)

**iPhone:** open the link in **Safari** → tap **Share** (square with arrow ↑) → **Add to Home
Screen** → **Add**. Open the new icon and wait until it says **“Ready for offline”**.

**Android:** open the link in **Chrome** → tap **⋮** (top right) → **Add to Home screen** (or
**Install app**) → **Add**. Open the new icon and wait until it says **“Ready for offline”**.

## 2 · Using it

* The buttons at the bottom: **Home** · **Plan** (day by day) · **Map** · **Say it** · **Help**.
* **Home** shows what is next and how long until it starts.
* Tap an **address** to copy it. **Call** buttons ring the number directly.
* Tap a **document** or **map** to see it big. Pinch with two fingers, or use **+** and **−**.
* **Tickets with a QR code:** open the ticket, zoom until the code fills the screen, and turn the
  screen brightness up before it is scanned.
* **Say it:** tap a card to show it in big letters. **▶ Play** says it out loud.
  On an event, the **Say it** button shows “Please take me to…” with the address, for taxi drivers.
* **Reminders** pop up 15 minutes before each booking, **but only while the app is open**.
  So open it now and then.
* **“Open in Maps”** needs internet. Without internet, use the app's own **Map** page and the
  addresses.

## 3 · Updates

If something changes, just open the app when you have Wi-Fi. It updates itself and says
**“Your trip was updated”**.

**Emergency: 112** (big red button on the Help page). **Call me:** the green button on the Help page.
