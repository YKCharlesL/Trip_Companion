All your bookings, tickets, maps and addresses in one place. It works without internet once it is set up.

Your link: {{LINK}}

1 · Set it up (once, at home on Wi-Fi)

iPhone
  1. Open the link in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button (square with an arrow ↑).
  3. Tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.
  4. Open the new icon on the home screen and wait until it says “Ready for offline”.
Android
  1. Open the link in Chrome.
  2. Tap the ⋮ menu (top right).
  3. Tap Add to Home screen (or Install app), then Add.
  4. Open the new icon and wait until it says “Ready for offline”.

2 · Using it

  • The buttons at the bottom: Home · Plan (day by day) · Map · Say it · Help.
  • Home shows what is next and how long until it starts.
  • Tap an address to copy it. Call buttons ring the number directly.
  • Tap a document or map to see it big. Pinch with two fingers, or use + and −, to zoom.
  • Tickets with a QR code: open the ticket, zoom until the code fills the screen, and turn the brightness up.
  • Say it: tap a card to show it in big letters. ▶ Play says it out loud. On an event, the Say it button shows “Please take me to…” with the address, for taxi drivers.
  • Reminders pop up 15 minutes before each booking, but only while the app is open. So open it now and then.
  • “Open in Maps” needs internet. Without internet, use the app’s own Map page and the addresses.

3 · Updates

If I change something, just open the app when you have Wi-Fi. It updates itself and says “Your trip was updated”.
